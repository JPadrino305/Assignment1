About Us

SINGULAR is a Miami-based luxury coffee house and micro-market that serves only the world’s rarest, traceable coffees: limited microlots, award-winning competition lots, experimental processes, and small harvests from distinct farms. Every coffee is offered as a temporary “release,” much like a gallery exhibition or fashion drop—once it is gone, it may never return. The brand treats coffee as a collectible agricultural craft, not an everyday commodity. A guest does not simply order “a latte”; they choose an origin, producer, process, and tasting experience.
